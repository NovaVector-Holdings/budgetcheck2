## The 5 key questions Gate 2 must answer

Ask these without coaching the concepts first — a participant's own unprompted words are the
evidence:

1. Do users spontaneously understand that this is about what the next paycheck needs to cover?
2. When asked what number matters most right now, do they identify **Estimated remaining**
   rather than current balance / debt / savings?
3. Does the experience feel like a credible financial product?
4. Do ALL five understand that Estimated remaining is: based on entered information; not a live
   bank balance; not guaranteed; not permission to spend?
5. Can they explain what BudgetChek is telling them to do / know next?

## Keep available / Buffer probe (diagnostic, Gate D-adjacent)

Because prior research exposed confusion here specifically, ask this neutral follow-up **without
explaining the answer first**:

> "In your own words, what's the difference between 'Keep available' and 'Buffer'?"

Expected understanding (record actual responses as diagnostic evidence, not a new mandatory gate):

- **Keep available:** money associated with identified items before payday.
- **Buffer:** additional money the person intentionally chose to leave untouched.

Do not create a new post-hoc mandatory threshold on this probe after responses begin — it is
diagnostic evidence alongside Gate E, not a sixth locked gate.
